Getting around

W A S D, arrow keys
Walk. On a touch screen, use the joystick
Drag
Look around
F
Switch between walking and flying
B
Next view: darkness, simple BIM, blob BIM, point cloud, real view

Finding things

Tap an object
See its record, and how sure we are of the link
L
Equipment by importance. Pick one to go to its best view
T
Filters: type, condition, and which alerts show
Map
The minimap. Click it for the full map
G
Plan a route to the faulty device

Moving an object

M, or Move
Start moving the selected object
Arrow keys
Move it, away from you and to the side. Shift goes further
Page Up, Page Down
Raise and lower it
R
Rotate instead. Left and right arrow keys turn it
Enter
Done. Ctrl+Z undoes the latest change

Val

Talk to Val, or type. Press again to send
Ask
"Why is P-005 at attention required?", "What should I check first?"
Tell
"Show only pumps", "Show the point cloud", "Take me to P-005", "Start the demo"
