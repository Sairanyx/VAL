# Challenge: Industrial Metaverse (Valmet)

**Semantic 3D Scene Understanding & Autonomous Field-Service Copilot**

Imagine entering a digital industrial plant where the environment does more than look real. It understands where machines are, what they are, how people or robots could move between them and which equipment needs attention. Today's 3D captures can be visually extraordinary—but they often lack the meaning needed for intelligent action. Valmet challenges you to bridge that gap. Explore how spatial perception, semantic 3D understanding and AI-grounded equipment information could transform a scanned industrial environment into a navigable, useful digital world. Think computer vision, point-cloud intelligence, digital twins, robotics simulation and an assistant that can explain the context behind a maintenance decision. This is a chance to make AI understand not just words and numbers, but physical space. A strong demonstration could point toward industrial operations where service experts and autonomous systems find the right equipment faster, navigate complex facilities more intelligently and turn fragmented technical data into confident, context-aware action.
